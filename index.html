About

I am currently a Staff Engineer at Samsung Research Center-Beijing (SRCB), working on embodied AI with a focus on robotic manipulation. I earned my Ph.D. under the supervision of Prof. Guohua Hu at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), where I published first-author papers in journals including Nature Communications. Before joining SRCB, I interned at Anker Innovations. My research interests focus on embodied AI, robotic manipulation, tactile sensing, and reinforcement learning.
